      Source. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information — station probabilities derived from the 1991–2020 U.S. Climate Normals (the table behind Climate.gov’s “Historical Probability of a White Christmas” map). Station spreadsheet · stations.csv

      Method. Station values are interpolated to a grid. The surface is this dashboard’s own interpolation, not NOAA’s, and it does not account for terrain, so mountain areas with sparse stations are smoothed. Climatology is not a forecast; check weather.gov within a week of the holiday.
