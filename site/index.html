      Map. The colored surface is NOAA’s own rendering of the 1991–2020 normals, served from NOAA’s Christmas_Snow_2020normals_Lambert_update map service in its native projection (North America Lambert Conformal Conic, ESRI:102009). NOAA publishes five zoom levels; the finest is about 500 m per pixel.

      Stations. Dots, search and click look-ups use the station values behind that map from NOAA NCEI (source · stations.csv). A click shows the nearest station, not an interpolated value. Climatology is not a forecast; check weather.gov within a week of the holiday.
